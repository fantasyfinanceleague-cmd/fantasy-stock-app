Three directions for Stockpile

Each direction is a complete identity for web and mobile: palette, type, a logo treatment, and one signature motion moment. Every one is shown on the same two screens: the weekly matchup (the product's core moment) and the landing hero. The data is illustrative, and matchups are won on dollar gain, as they are in the real scorer.

Pick one direction, or tell me what to borrow from another. Whichever you choose, these stay fixed: one token source per platform, WCAG AA contrast on every data colour, a reduced-motion fallback for every animation, and the navigation plan in DESIGN_DIRECTION.md.

Direction A · light · calm fintech

Ledger

Stockpile as a well-made financial paper. Numbers are typeset in a serif the way a stock page would print them, and everything else stays quiet: cool white, one deep harbor-teal accent, hairlines instead of shadows. Motion only happens when a number changes. It feels like Public and the Financial Times more than a game.

Direction B · light base with broadcast scoreboards · calm by default, loud on game day Recommended

Game Day

Your portfolio screens stay calm and light, like a fintech app. The competitive surfaces (matchup, standings, draft, results) switch into a broadcast scoreboard: stadium navy, condensed numerals, your team colour against your opponent's, a live tug-of-war, and chyrons that call the play ("NVDA just put you ahead"). The investing side stays credible, and the game side gets the energy and animation you asked for.

Direction C · dark · premium terminal

After Hours

A dark, precise, pro-tool feel: deep blue-slate surfaces layered by elevation, hairline borders, prices in a monospace, and one iris accent for interaction. The matchup is two lines racing on one chart, with the leader's line lit. Motion is fast and physical: springs, shared-element transitions from a row into its detail, and a quiet glow where data is live. It feels like Linear or a modern trading terminal.
