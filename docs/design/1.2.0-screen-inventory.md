## Legacy or unowned: what decides 1.2.0

1. **`create-league`** (legacy, 74 colour uses) — owned by 3c, **not built**. Season and Draft steps, pick clock, draft order mode, the uneven-bye heads-up and the playoff-team rule are all in the 3c spec. This is 3c's biggest remaining gap.
2. **`league-settings`** (legacy, 52) — owned by 3c, **not restyled**. The spec's league-settings frames and the freeze-slot copy are still to do.
3. **`join-league`** (legacy, 23) — **nobody owns it** and it has no board frame. It is the path for a new player with an invite code, so it cannot be left on legacy styling.
4. **`+not-found`** (legacy) — **nobody owns it**. It is shown for any bad deep link, so it needs a new-UI screen.
5. **`modal`** (template) and **`design-gallery`** (dev) — **nobody**. Remove `modal`; confirm `design-gallery` is excluded from release builds.
6. **`draft`** (legacy route) — kept for the finalize heal. Retire it once the heal moves to the room.
7. **3e screens** (`portfolio`, `player-portfolio`, `trade-history`) — legacy or placeholder until 3e merges. They are 3e's; this document does not judge them further.

**Answer:** yes, 1.2.0 needs another worker for three things nobody owns: `join-league`, `+not-found`, and the `modal` / `design-gallery` cleanup. 3c still has `create-league` and `league-settings` to bring onto the new UI. 3e's three screens decide the rest.
