# Naming: a new name for "Stockpile"

> Author: Design Lead. Date: 2026-09-26. Branch: `docs/naming`.
> Status: **SHORTLIST FOR GIORGIO. He picks; this document doesn't.**
>
> Why: "Stockpile" is taken in our exact category. stockpile.com is a US
> investing app (since 2010) that uses "stockpile™" and an orange S-shaped
> mark (found by the Orchestrator, 2026-09-26).
>
> **This is an informal screen, not legal clearance.** It records what public
> web, app-store and domain sources show on 2026-09-26. Before committing to a
> name, Giorgio should run the USPTO queries below himself and, ideally, get a
> trademark attorney's clearance opinion. "No results found" here means
> "nothing indexed", not "free to use".

---

## 1. Brief

**What it is.** A fantasy league for real stocks. Friends form a league, hold
a snake **draft** of real tickers, then play **weekly head-to-head matchups**
across a **season** with standings and playoffs. A week is won by the bigger
**dollar gain** from Monday's open to Friday's close. Free to play, no real
money: it tracks performance and never buys, sells or holds securities.

**Audience.** Friend groups who already do fantasy sports and follow markets
(roughly 18–35), plus recruiters and investors who see the landing page.

**Tone.** Game Day (DESIGN_DIRECTION.md): calm and credible on the money side,
broadcast energy on the competitive side. The name should sound like a
**game you play with friends** before it sounds like a finance product.

**Constraints.**
- Short, easy to say and spell after hearing it once; works as an App Store
  name (30-character limit) and as an icon.
- **Must not imply real-money brokerage, investing services or advice**:
  avoid "invest", "capital", "trade", "broker", "fund", "wealth", "advisor".
- Distinct from a crowded field. Same-category products found during this
  screen: Fantasy Stocks (FS Digital, weekly lineups decided at the Friday
  close), FantasyFunds (weekly 1v1), Fantasy Sharks (weekly head-to-head on
  P/L), PlayFSL (Fantasy Stock Leagues), StockDraft (daily fantasy stocks),
  GameStock, BullRush, UpTick, Fantasy Stock League (fantasystockleague.net).
  **Anything built from "fantasy" + "stock" is effectively unownable.** A
  coined or double-meaning name is the realistic path to something
  protectable.

## 3. Shortlist: rationale and informal availability screen

Domains were checked on 2026-09-26: `.com` via Verisign whois, `.app` via
Google Registry RDAP (a control lookup, google.app, returned 200 as expected).
"Registered" doesn't mean in use; sites were probed where registered. **Social
handles can't be verified without signing in to each platform**; no indexed
accounts were found for the top three, but check @name on X, Instagram and
TikTok by hand.

### 3.1 Tickerball

**Rationale.** A coined compound, so it's the strongest trademark position on
the list. It says both halves of the product in three syllables: ticker (the
market) and ball (the game). It's playful, not financial, so it can't be
mistaken for a brokerage. The icon writes itself (a ball), and the refined
bars fit inside it.

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Same-category web | No product named Tickerball. **Sound-alike: TickerBell** (tickerbell.com), a stock research and analysis platform, in adjacent category 9/36 territory. A dissolved Florida LLC "Tickerball, LLC" (2007–2008) |
| App Store / Play | No app named Tickerball. Near: "Ticker Sports" (scores), "Tigerball" (game) |
| Domains | tickerball.com **registered 2021-06-14**, no site responds (parked). **tickerball.app: not registered.** playtickerball.com: not registered |
| Handles | None indexed |
| Collision flag | **TickerBell** (name: TickerBell; category: stock research; mark: not visible in public page text). Risk is phonetic similarity in a related class. **The main thing the USPTO search must answer** |

### 3.2 Friday Close

**Rationale.** It names the product's defining moment: every matchup is
decided at Friday's close. It reads like an event ("see you at Friday Close")
and suits the Game Day reveal. Weaknesses: it's **descriptive**, which makes a
weaker trademark; it's two words; and holiday weeks end on a Thursday.

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Same-category web | No product named Friday Close |
| App Store / Play | No app of that name (only generic "Friday" apps) |
| Domains | **fridayclose.com registered 2026-09-25, the day before this screen**, behind Cloudflare (SSL error 526, so a site is being set up). **fridayclose.app: not registered** |
| Handles | None indexed |
| Collision flag | No mark collision found. **The domain was registered yesterday by an unknown party**, so someone may be building on this name right now. Treat it as contested |

### 3.3 TickerDraft

**Rationale.** Plain and descriptive: you draft tickers. It's instantly
understood, but it names the **draft** (once a season) rather than the weekly
game you actually play, and "Draft" is crowded in fantasy apps (FanDraft,
Market Draft).

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Same-category web | No product named TickerDraft. Near: **Market Draft** (stock-market simulation app) and **StockDraft** (daily fantasy stocks) |
| App Store / Play | None of that name |
| Domains | **tickerdraft.com registered 2025-12-17**, no site responds. **tickerdraft.app: not registered** |
| Handles | None indexed |
| Collision flag | Conceptual crowding with StockDraft / Market Draft (same category, "draft" + stock term). A recent unknown .com registration |

### 3.4 Longball (risky)

**Rationale.** The best double meaning after Pennant: "going long" on a stock
and hitting the long ball. It's short and punchy.

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Same-category web | **Longball Capital** (analytics and capital for athletes and teams: sports **and** finance, class 36 adjacent). "Long Ball" football-fan app (removed from Play in 2025) |
| Domains | longball.com registered 2010; longball.app registered |
| Collision flag | **Longball Capital**: name Longball, category sports finance, sits exactly where we'd be |

### 3.5 Cap Space (risky)

**Rationale.** Salary-cap space (roster building) meets market cap; it fits
the draft and budget mechanics.

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Same-category web | **Capspace**, an Australian commercial lender and investment firm (finance, class 36); Capspace screenshot app (class 9) |
| Domains | capspace.com registered 2000; capspace.app registered |
| Collision flag | **Capspace (AU lender)**: same name, finance category |

### 3.6 Pennant (risky)

**Rationale.** The richest meaning: the championship flag every league plays
for, and the pennant chart pattern traders know. A beautiful name, but crowded.

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Same-category web | **Pennant Chase**, a fantasy baseball league site with commissioner tools (1,800+ leagues); **"Pennant"**, an iOS sports-standings app; **PennantPark Investment** (finance, ticker PNNT) |
| Domains | pennant.com registered 1995; pennant.app registered |
| Collision flag | **Pennant Chase** (fantasy leagues, class 41) and the **Pennant** app (sports, class 9). It's the most-used name on the list |

## 5. Rename impact (what it costs)

### Cheap: tokens and copy

- `brand.name` / `brand.wordmark` are single tokens in Phase 2 (both
  platforms), so new UI renames by editing one value.
- Existing hard-coded "Stockpile": **47 occurrences in 20 files**:
  web `index.html`, `Header.jsx`, `Login.jsx`, `OnboardingModal.jsx`,
  `utils/errorMessages.js`, `LandingPage.jsx/.css`,
  `styles/stockpile-tokens.css`; mobile `app/login.tsx`, `app/(tabs)/index.tsx`,
  `app/(tabs)/draft.tsx`, and comments in `constants/theme/*`. Phase 3 replaces
  most of these anyway.
- Image assets named for the brand: `apps/mobile/assets/images/{Stockpile_Logo,
  stockpile-logo,stockpile-icon-only,stockpile-logo-light-full}.png`, plus
  `icon.png`, `adaptive-icon.png`, `splash-icon.png`, `favicon.png`;
  `apps/web/public/{stockpile-icon.png,favicon.ico}`; `docs/design/*.png`.

### Needs a migration (backend, not a UI worker)

- The signup-refusal message lives **inside a SQL function**:
  `supabase/migrations/20260815000000_signup_gate.sql:115`, "Stockpile is not
  open for new signups yet…". Changing it needs a new migration
  (`CREATE OR REPLACE`) and a `db push` (human). Two other mentions are
  comments only (`20260810000003`, `functions/quote/index.ts:3`).

### Not cheap: native identifiers (`apps/mobile/app.json`, `eas.json`)

| Field | Current value | Rename impact |
|---|---|---|
| `expo.name` (home-screen / display name) | **`fantasy-stock-app`**, so the icon label today isn't even "Stockpile" | Worth fixing whatever the name. Baked into the native build, so it needs a **new binary** (not OTA) |
| `expo.slug` | `fantasy-stock-app` | **Keep.** Tied to EAS project `762da87e-578d-4041-ae85-37d8aa312187`; renaming it mismatches the project. Not user-visible |
| `expo.scheme` | `fantasystockapp` | **Keep** (or add a second scheme). Deep links, and possibly the password-reset / auth redirect URLs configured in Supabase, use it; changing it breaks links already in inboxes. Not user-visible |
| `ios.bundleIdentifier` | `com.stockpile.fantasystock` | **Recommend KEEP.** Invisible to users. Changing it makes a **new app**: a new App Store Connect record, TestFlight testers must install a different app, and existing installs, push tokens and keychain sessions don't carry over |
| `android.package` | **not set** | Choose it **once, now, name-neutral** (e.g. `com.<studio>.app`) before the first Android build. It can never change after Play publication |
| `updates.url` / OTA channels | EAS project URL | Unaffected by a display-name change |

### Note, don't change (per the Orchestrator)

- Web domain: currently `fantasy-stock-app.vercel.app` (Vercel project name).
  A new name wants its own domain (see §3 for availability).
- Supabase project name, GitHub org `fantasyfinanceleague-cmd` and repo
  `fantasy-stock-app`: cosmetic, internal.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Supabase Auth email templates and the sender name (password
  reset, confirmations) may say "Stockpile". Check in the dashboard.

## 6. Recommendation (Giorgio decides)

**Tickerball**, with **Friday Close** as the alternative.

- *Tickerball* is the only shortlist name that is coined (the best trademark
  footing), playful rather than financial (it can't read as a brokerage), says
  both halves of the product, and has a free `.app`, a free `playtickerball.com`
  and a natural icon. Its one real question is **TickerBell**, and the USPTO
  search answers that. The `.com` is parked (since 2021), so it can be
  pursued later if the product takes off.
- *Friday Close* is the most meaningful to how the game works and the best
  "event" name, but it's descriptive (a weaker mark), and its `.com` was
  registered **yesterday** by someone else.
- I'd drop *TickerDraft* (it names the once-a-season draft, and the space is
  crowded) and treat *Longball*, *Cap Space* and *Pennant* as "love the
  meaning, can't own it".

Whatever the choice: keep the bundle ID, set a name-neutral Android package
now, fix the "fantasy-stock-app" home-screen name in the next binary, and let
the `brand.name` token carry the rest.
